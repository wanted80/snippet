# Notes from a patient oven


Bread makes time visible. The dough changes while nothing appears to happen, and rushing it only hides the lesson.

1. Mix flour, water, salt, and starter.
2. Fold the dough several times.
3. Leave it alone.
4. Bake until the crust is deeply browned.

The final loaf was uneven and a little too dense. It was also gone before Sunday afternoon.
