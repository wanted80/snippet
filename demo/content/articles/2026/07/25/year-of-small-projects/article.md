# A month-by-month making log


The rule was simple: each project had to fit inside one month and teach one unfamiliar skill.

## What got made

- A weather display for an old tablet
- A tiny database of neighborhood trees
- A printed booklet of family recipes
- A static site generator for personal notes

Not every project survived the month. The useful habit was learning to declare something **finished**, write down what it taught, and begin again.
