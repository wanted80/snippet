# Lessons the recipe leaves out


Recipes remember ingredients well but often forget the conditions around them. A note that says “use less water on a humid day” can be more valuable than another page of instructions.

My notebook is full of ordinary observations: the pan that runs hot, the loaf that likes a longer rest, and the soup that improves after an overnight pause. None of them are definitive. Together, they make cooking feel more conversational.

The best notes leave room for the next attempt to disagree.
