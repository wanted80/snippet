# Small changes in the day


The forecast is never the whole story. A cool window beside the desk, a bright patch on the kitchen floor, or rain beginning during a walk can quietly rearrange the day.

I have started recording these details alongside larger notes. They are not important events, but they make the record feel inhabited. A sentence about work reads differently when it also remembers the afternoon thunder.

Paying attention to weather is a small way to notice time passing without turning every day into a performance.
