# An archive should welcome you back


An archive becomes useful when it is easy to enter. I do not need every note to have a perfect category; I need a stable place to put it and a title that gives my future self a chance.

The simplest structure has held up best: dates for sequence, descriptive names for subjects, and a few tags for the connections that are worth remembering. The gaps and oddities are part of the history.

Tidiness is helpful, but recoverability is the real goal.
