# Questions as signposts


When a project feels too large, I try not to reach for a larger plan. I write down a better question instead. “What is the smallest useful version?” is usually more helpful than “How should this work?”

Other questions act like signposts: Who is this for? What can be removed? What would make the result feel finished? They do not solve the work, but they make the next step visible.

Good questions are a form of navigation. They turn a foggy landscape into a handful of paths worth walking.
