# Rules that make a project feel like itself


A personal project still benefits from a few repeated decisions. The trick is to make those decisions small enough to remember and flexible enough to survive contact with real content.

I started with three rules: one body typeface, a short spacing scale, and a single accent color. Everything else can remain a local choice. The result feels less like a template and more like a room where the furniture belongs together.

The system is useful precisely because it is modest. It gives attention to the words instead of asking the author to maintain a miniature design department.
