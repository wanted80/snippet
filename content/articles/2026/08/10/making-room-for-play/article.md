# Experiments without an obligation


Not every project needs a useful outcome. Some are valuable because they make a familiar tool behave strangely, or because they give an afternoon a shape that work would not have found.

I keep a short list of experiments with intentionally low stakes. Change the colors. Rewrite the interface in a different voice. Make a tiny version and stop before it becomes an obligation.

Play is not a reward for finishing serious work. Sometimes it is the quickest way to remember why making things is enjoyable.
