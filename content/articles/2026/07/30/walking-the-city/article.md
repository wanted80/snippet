# A longer route through the city


The direct route takes twenty minutes. On Friday I ignored it and followed a street I had passed for years without entering.

There was a repair shop with hand-painted lettering, a tiny garden growing behind a fence, and a bakery closing for the afternoon.

## Then the weather changed

Rain arrived all at once. Five strangers gathered beneath the same narrow awning and watched the empty street shine.

Sometimes getting lost is just another way of paying attention.
