# Familiar tools leave room


A new project already contains uncertainty. The problem, the audience, and even the reason for building it may change.

Choosing familiar tools leaves more room for those questions.

## What boring means here

- The runtime is easy to install.
- The data is readable without special software.
- Failures produce messages a person can act on.
- The finished result has few moving parts.

**Boring is not the absence of craft.** It is where the craft can become visible.
